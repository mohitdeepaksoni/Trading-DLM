### 5.2 What is unique — the combination, not any single piece
1. **Demand-system clearing + a computable general-equilibrium prior.** Koijen–Yogo demand systems exist; Agora-class
   CGE engines exist; no trading system couples them so that a tariff or macro shock enters asset pricing through
   solved second-round sector effects. Measured: the CGE price channel explains the day-0 sector cross-section (p=0.004).
2. **The pricing kernel derived from the same investors that clear the market**, so no-arbitrage and market clearing
   are one object; and the law imposed at the model's equilibrium price rather than the observed price. The second
   point is, as far as we can find, not stated in the deep-asset-pricing literature, and it is the difference between a
   model that keeps a mispricing signal (0.955) and one that inverts it (−0.50).
3. **An identification theorem that decides the architecture.** Prices identify (μ, κ) only; therefore state comes
   from holdings and beliefs from measured fundamentals or expectations, and the network's job is deliberately small.
   Most DL-in-finance work adds capacity where we removed it.
4. **Honesty as a component, not a chapter.** Procedure-hashed trials, versioned scopes, validator validation,
   point-in-time universes, revision rules, and a pre-registered, Bitcoin-anchored daily record. Two rungs that pass
   every single-strategy gate and fail the search-level PBO by 0.008 are handled by the live record, not by another
   backtest.
5. **Negative results kept.** Kronos zero-shot, volatility gating, holdings levels as signals, and the v0 learned
   corrections are all on the ledger as rejected; the product is whatever survives, which today is a no-fit composite,
   an accruals anchor, and a validated structural event channel.

(results + shareholding), and seconds on any rerun. A better GPU would not have changed this; a profiler did.
