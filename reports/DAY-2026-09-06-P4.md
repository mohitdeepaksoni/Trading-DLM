## Finding 1 — with a fundamentals anchor, structure alone finds the mispricing
On synthetic markets with 5 % injected mispricing, the untrained model (beliefs from a fundamentals-level feature, default investor state, closed-form clearing) recovers the mispricing with correlation **0.95** and beliefs with 0.92. No learning involved: this is the clearing model doing the work.

## Finding 2 — the moment residual does not identify the investor state at this scale
Training with R1 as the only teacher (unpenalised) drives R1 to its noise floor but **degrades** the mispricing correlation (0.95 → 0.10) and never recovers risk tolerance A or index share φ (correlations ≈ 0). Price-anchored beliefs (the natural choice without fundamentals data) give **no** mispricing identification at all (corr −0.1 to −0.2). Identification-vs-sample-size sweep (A10G, R1 the only teacher, corrections unpenalised, 1,500 steps):
| dates × assets | moment obs. | mispricing corr init → trained | beliefs corr | A corr | φ corr | R1 init → trained |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,024 × 8 | 49 k | 0.95 → 0.72 | 0.60 | −0.02 | −0.03 | 0.014 → 0.009 |
| 8,192 × 8 | 393 k | 0.95 → 0.56 | 0.45 | +0.01 | +0.03 | 0.034 → 0.024 |
| 32,768 × 8 | 1.6 M | 0.95 → 0.63 | 0.50 | +0.03 | −0.02 | 0.023 → 0.011 |
| 8,192 × 32 | 1.6 M | 0.94 → 0.56 | 0.43 | 0.00 | +0.04 | 0.063 → 0.004 |
| 32,768 × 32 | 6.3 M | 0.94 → 0.55 | 0.38 | +0.03 | −0.04 | 0.067 → 0.004 |
**This is not a sample-size problem.** R1 falls by an order of magnitude while the state stays unidentified and the
signal degrades at every scale: inside E[M D] = p, beliefs μ and the kernel's risk term (q/A) trade off exactly, so
prices and returns alone cannot separate "the market expects less" from "the market demands more compensation".
One of the two must be anchored from outside the price system — beliefs from fundamentals, or the state from
holdings and flows. That is the mathematical reason the Market Accounting Matrix and the nowcaster are not
optional components but the identification device of the whole model.
**Consequences, now encoded:** beliefs must be anchored to fundamentals-level information (earnings/dividends, or Agora's shock-implied payoff change); the investor state comes from the nowcaster (holdings and flows), not from R1; the net's corrections are penalised toward zero and R1 is a *consistency check* that may only move them when the law demands. This is doc 07 A.1 and doc 08 §2 confirmed by experiment, and it removes a failure mode before any capital touches it.

## Monte Carlo in this engine (question raised today)
Used where an expectation has no closed form or where the null distribution is the object: the synthetic market
pool, the stationary bootstrap, the permutation null, later the path simulations for drawdown/Kelly and the
neural-SDE generator. Removed where it was adding noise: the pricing-kernel normaliser is Gaussian and now analytic

## Evening — the identification issue taken seriously, and resolved structurally (research/09)
1. **Theorem-level statement** (numerically pinned): returns identify beliefs μ and the composite κ = (1−φ)/A, never A and φ separately; κ converges slowly (corr 0.6 after one year of daily data, 0.86 after four). φ comes from holdings; A is a nuisance for pricing. The model now carries κ as its state, seeded by a structural estimator on the trailing window.
2. **The deeper cause of the degradation**: the pricing law was imposed at the observed price, which makes "no mispricing" the objective. Imposed at the model's equilibrium price, training preserves the out-of-sample mispricing signal exactly (0.955 unpenalised, 0.953 penalised) instead of destroying it (0.11). This is now the default, with validation early stopping.
3. Tests: 10 P4 tests green (34 total). The analytic-kernel sweep on the A10 (old formulation) reproduced the degradation at every scale, as expected — it is the pre-fix baseline.

## Emporion v0 on real US data — first walk-forward (train < 2021, validate 2021, test 2022→2026, 55 test months)
Inputs per date: value anchor (log fair price, clipped), SUE, accruals, momentum, low-risk, size, on the point-in-time
top-200; return units (prices normalised to one, 20-day payoffs, covariance of 20-day returns, cap-weight supply); κ
from the structural estimator (winsorised); law imposed at the equilibrium price; validation early stopping.
Two numerical faults were found and fixed on the way (price-unit kernel exponent overflowing; the trainer's default
soft-consistency weight). Result with corrections penalised toward zero: test RankIC **+0.030 (t 1.50, hit 0.62)** for
−r_mkt versus **+0.030 (t 1.50)** for the value anchor alone — the model reproduces its anchor and adds nothing; the
law is satisfiable without moving beliefs because 20-day realised payoffs are dominated by noise. Variants with the
penalties released and checkpoint selection on validation rank-IC (model selection, never the loss) follow below.
Variants (all test 2022→2026, 55 months): penalties 0.1 with law-residual selection **+0.029 (t 1.45)**; penalties 0.1 with
validation-rank-IC selection **+0.030 (t 1.50)**; penalties 0.01 with rank-IC selection **+0.030 (t 1.45)** — against
**+0.030 (t 1.50)** for the value anchor alone. Conclusion: on 38 monthly training cross-sections the network cannot
learn belief corrections from the law; the informative anchors (accruals) are present as features but the law gives
no gradient to use them. This is the "small learning problem" of research/09 seen from the other side: with prices
identifying only μ and κ, and κ needing years, 38 dates are not enough to learn anything beyond the anchor. Next: (i) a
fixed equal-weight composite of the standardised anchors as a no-fit rung, (ii) 5-day decision dates for ~20× the
cross-sections, (iii) pooled training across US + NSE + synthetic pre-training.

## Investor-state nowcaster (P3c) — `emporion/structure/nowcaster.py`
Kalman filter per asset: state = institutional share, exact at filing dates, random walk with a bounded innovation
between them, drift from a flow proxy with a loading fitted on filing-to-filing changes. Tests: recovers the loading,
tracks the state, exact at filings, no look-ahead, variance bounded, and beats last-value carry-forward on held-out
filings when flows are informative. Ready to take 13F (US) and shareholding + FII/DII (NSE) as inputs; the filtered
variance is the band that clamps the encoder's investor-state head.
